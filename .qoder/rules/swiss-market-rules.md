# Swiss Market Business Rules

## Currency Formatting (ALWAYS)
- Display: "CHF 45.50" (with space)
- Calculations: Use Swiss franc precision (2 decimals)
- Minimum values: CHF 20 for auctions
- Increments: CHF 5 for bids

## Regional Standards
- Timezone: Europe/Zurich for all user-facing times
- Language: German, French, Italian, English support
- Address format: Swiss postal codes (4 digits)
- Phone format: +41 XX XXX XX XX

## Business Hours
- Standard: 08:00–18:00 Monday-Friday
- Weekend: 09:00–17:00 Saturday, closed Sunday
- Holiday handling: Swiss federal holidays

## Auction Rules
- Minimum starting price: CHF 20
- Bid increment: CHF 5
- Maximum duration: 24 hours
- Auto-close: When expired or reserve met

## Service Categories (Swiss Context)
- Handwerk (Craftsmanship)
- Reparaturen (Repairs)  
- Installation
- Wartung (Maintenance)
- Notdienst (Emergency service)

## Compliance
- Swiss data protection laws
- Consumer protection standards
- Service provider licensing requirements
